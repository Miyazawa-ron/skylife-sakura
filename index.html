philosophy理念

designing the region

we redefine space — not as property, but as a place where people and communities connect.

sky ライフ co., ltd. breathes new life into existing buildings and overlooked spaces, turning them into homes for travelers, long-term residents, and the communities around them.

design is at the heart of everything we do — not as decoration, but as a way of shaping how people live. our guiding principle: designing the region and the way we live. わたしたちは空間を「所有」ではなく、人とまちがつながる「場所」として捉え直します。設計は装飾ではなく、暮らしのかたちそのものです。
